# Distributor code of conduct

*Last updated: 10 August 2026*

The publisher of The Microcap Minute Global is separately a registered distributor of
Portfolio Management Services in India. That is a different activity from this
publication, conducted with different people under different rules, and this page sets out
the rules it is conducted under. The registration details follow at the foot of the page.

## The separation, first

**The Microcap Minute Global is not a distribution activity.**

The newsletter is impersonal commentary of general and regular circulation, covering
securities listed on exchanges outside India. It does not distribute, market, solicit or
recommend any Portfolio Management Service, any scheme, or any product for which the
publisher is a distributor. No portfolio manager pays for coverage in it, sponsors it,
contributes to it, or has any say in what appears.

**Nothing in the newsletter earns the publisher distribution commission.** The companies
that appear in an issue are selected by the tests published in our
[Methodology](methodology.html), applied to market data. Whether the publisher distributes
anything, and to whom, has no bearing on that process at any stage.

Subscription revenue from the newsletter and any commission earned as a distributor are
separate income from separate activities with separate customers, and this page is the
only place on this site where the second one is discussed.

## The code of conduct we follow

Where distribution does take place, and only with the persons to whom it takes place, the
Code of Conduct for PMS Distributors set by the Securities and Exchange Board of India
applies in full. These are the commitments it creates.

**The investor's interest comes first, in all circumstances.** Their interest and the
suitability of a strategy to their financial needs are the starting point of every
conversation, not a check applied at the end of one.

**All commissions are disclosed.** Any person to whom a Portfolio Management Service is
distributed is told all material information about it, including every commission received
for soliciting that strategy, before they commit to anything.

**No returns are indicated or assured.** Not in conversation, not in writing, not by
implication, for any strategy, ever.

**Commission never drives a recommendation.** A higher commission on one strategy than
another is not a reason to put anybody into it. No commission is rebated to an investor,
and no gift or inducement of any kind is offered to attract one.

**Every relationship is classified at the outset** as either advisory or execution only,
and that classification is disclosed to the person concerned rather than left implied.

**Comparisons are made only against similar and comparable products, with complete facts.**
We do not make negative statements about any other portfolio manager or strategy.

**Sales and promotional material follows SEBI's requirements**, including its rules on how
performance may be presented. Where a strategy is discussed, the current performance
report, risk factors, portfolio strategy and any deviations are provided as SEBI defines
them, and assumptions are stated rather than buried.

**Investor information is confidential.** Details, dealings and transactions are not
discussed with anyone who is not entitled to them.

**High standards of ethics, integrity and fairness apply to every party**, including
investors, portfolio managers, advisers and other intermediaries.

The Code is published by SEBI as Annexure A3 to its Portfolio Managers framework and is
public. APMI maintains the distributor register and may de-register a distributor for
breaching the Code or for mis-selling.

### What this registration is, and is not

**A distributor registration is not an advisory registration.** It permits the holder to
distribute portfolio management services. It does not make the holder an Investment
Adviser, a Research Analyst, or a Portfolio Manager, and this publication does not act as
any of those. Ayush Agrawal is not registered with SEBI as an Investment Adviser or as a
Research Analyst.

**This page solicits nothing.** Nobody reading it is being offered a Portfolio Management
Service, and no such service is described, named or promoted anywhere on this site or in
the newsletter. If you want to know whether a distribution relationship would suit you,
that is a separate conversation with a separate set of documents, and it begins at the
address above rather than here.
