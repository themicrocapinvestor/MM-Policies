# Distributor disclosure

*Last updated: 10 August 2026*

This page exists because the publisher of The Microcap Minute Global holds a registration
that readers are entitled to know about, and because the two activities must not be
confused with one another.

## The registration

**Ayush Agrawal is a Portfolio Management Services distributor registered with the
Association of Portfolio Managers in India (APMI).**

**APMI Registration Number: APRN09522**

APMI is the industry body recognised by the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI)
for portfolio managers. Since 1 January 2025, any person or entity distributing Portfolio
Management Services in India must be registered with APMI, under SEBI's circular of 2 May
2024 on collective oversight of PMS distributors.

**A distributor registration is not an advisory registration.** It permits the holder to
distribute portfolio management services. It does not make the holder an Investment
Adviser, a Research Analyst, or a Portfolio Manager, and this publication does not act as
any of those. Ayush Agrawal is not registered with SEBI as an Investment Adviser or as a
Research Analyst.

## The two things are separate, and this is the important part

**The Microcap Minute Global is not a distribution activity.**

The newsletter is impersonal commentary of general and regular circulation, covering
securities listed on exchanges outside India. It does not distribute, market, solicit or
recommend any Portfolio Management Service, any scheme, or any product for which the
publisher is a distributor. No portfolio manager pays for coverage in it, sponsors it,
contributes to it, or has any say in what appears.

**Nothing in the newsletter earns the publisher distribution commission.** The companies
that appear in an issue are selected by the tests published in our
[Methodology](methodology.html), applied to market data. Whether the publisher distributes
anything, and to whom, has no bearing on that process at any stage.

Subscription revenue from the newsletter and any commission earned as a distributor are
separate sources of income from separate activities with separate customers.

## What we disclose as a distributor

Where distribution does take place, and only with the persons to whom it takes place, the
SEBI Code of Conduct for PMS Distributors applies in full. In particular:

**All commissions are disclosed.** Any person to whom a Portfolio Management Service is
distributed is told all material information about it, including every commission received
for soliciting that strategy, before they commit to anything.

**No returns are indicated or assured.** Not in conversation, not in writing, not by
implication, for any strategy.

**Commission never drives a recommendation.** The investor's interest and the suitability
of a strategy to their needs come first. A higher commission on one strategy than another
is not a reason to put anybody into it, and no commission is rebated, and no gift or
inducement is offered to attract anyone.

**Every distribution relationship is classified** at the outset as either advisory or
execution only, and that classification is disclosed to the person concerned.

**Comparisons are made only against comparable products, with complete facts**, and we do
not make negative statements about other portfolio managers or their strategies.

The full Code of Conduct is published by SEBI as Annexure A3 to its Portfolio Managers
framework and is publicly available.

## What this page does not do

It does not solicit anything. Nobody reading this page is being offered a Portfolio
Management Service, and no such service is described, named or promoted anywhere on this
site or in the newsletter.

If you want to know whether a distribution relationship would suit you, that is a separate
conversation with a separate set of documents, and it begins by writing to the address
below. It does not begin here.

## Complaints

A complaint about any distribution activity may be made to
[hello@themicrocapminute.in](mailto:hello@themicrocapminute.in), and separately to APMI,
which maintains the distributor register and may de-register a distributor for breach of
the Code of Conduct or for mis-selling.

Complaints about the newsletter are handled under our
[Customer Support](support.html) policy, where the Grievance Officer is also named.
