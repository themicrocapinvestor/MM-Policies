# Cancellation and Refund Policy

*Last updated: 10 August 2026*

This policy covers cancellations and refunds for paid subscriptions to **The Microcap
Minute** and **The Microcap Minute Global**, published by Ayush Agrawal.

Contact: **[hello@themicrocapminute.in](mailto:hello@themicrocapminute.in)**

## Cancelling

**You can cancel at any time, for any reason, without telling us why.**

Cancel from your Substack account settings, under Subscriptions, or email us and we will
do it for you. There is no cancellation fee, no notice period, and
no retention process to sit through.

Cancellation takes effect **at the end of the period you have already paid for**. You
keep full access until then. We do not cut you off on the day you cancel.

After that date you are not billed again. Cancelling stops future payments; it is not by
itself a request for a refund of the current period. If you want that too, ask.

## Free subscriptions

Nothing to cancel and nothing to pay. Every issue carries a one-click unsubscribe link
that works immediately.

## Refunds

**Within 7 days of a payment, we refund in full on request.** No reason needed and no
questions asked. This applies to your first payment and to every renewal.

**After 7 days**, tell us what happened. Refunds are handled case by case and we lean
toward granting them. We would rather return your money than keep a subscriber who feels
badly treated.

**We refund in full, at any time, without being asked, if:**

- We charged you in error, or charged you twice
- Your subscription renewed after you had already cancelled
- We stop publishing during a period you have paid for

## Renewals

Paid subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled. Substack emails you before an
annual renewal.

**If an annual renewal catches you by surprise, tell us and we will refund it.** That
holds even outside the 7-day window. Nobody should pay for a year they did not intend to
buy.

## Smart Investor Tracker alerts (paid through Zoho)

Tracker email alerts are paid for **once, not on a renewal**, so the renewal parts of this
policy do not apply to them. Everything else does:

- **Within 7 days of payment, we refund in full on request**, no reason needed
- **After 7 days**, tell us what happened; we handle it case by case and lean toward
  granting refunds
- If we stop sending the alerts during a year you have paid for, **we refund the unused
  part in full, without being asked**
- The data in the alerts is the same data shown free on the tracker pages, so we do not
  refund on the basis that the information was available free; that is stated before you
  pay, and the payment is for delivery

Alert refunds are issued through **Zoho Payments** (not Stripe) to the original payment
method, typically 5 to 10 working days depending on your bank.

## Price changes

If we change the price, existing subscribers are told in advance and the change never
applies to a period already paid for. If you do not want to continue at a new price,
cancel before it takes effect.

## How to ask

Email **[hello@themicrocapminute.in](mailto:hello@themicrocapminute.in)** from the
address you subscribed with, or tell us which address it was.

We reply within **three working days**. Approved refunds are issued through Stripe to the original
payment method, and how quickly the money appears is down to your bank or card issuer,
typically **5 to 10 working days**. We cannot speed that part up.

## What we do not refund

We do not refund on the basis that the newsletter's content did not perform as you hoped.

**This publication is impersonal commentary. It carries no recommendations, no ratings,
no price targets and no forecasts, and it does not predict future returns.** Investment
outcomes are not something we sell, so they are not something we can refund. This is set
out in our [Terms of Service](terms.html) and in the disclaimer of every issue.

If you are dissatisfied with the quality, usefulness, or frequency of the writing itself,
that is a different matter. Tell us and we will look at it.

## Chargebacks

If something has gone wrong, please email us before raising a chargeback with your bank.
We can almost always resolve it faster, and a chargeback usually results in the
subscription being closed automatically.

## Complaints

If a refund decision seems wrong, reply and ask for it to be reviewed. The publisher
reads these personally.
