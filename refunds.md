# Cancellation and Refund Policy

*Last updated: 23 August 2026*

This policy covers cancellations and refunds for support given to **The Microcap
Minute** and **The Microcap Minute Global**, and for the legacy **Smart Investor Tracker
email alerts**.

Contact: **[hello@themicrocapminute.in](mailto:hello@themicrocapminute.in)**

## The newsletter is free

Every edition is free for every reader. There is no paywall and no subscription, so
there is nothing to cancel. Every issue carries a one-click unsubscribe link that works
immediately.

## Supporting the letter (Buy Me a Coffee)

If the work has been worth something to you, you can buy a coffee at
**[buymeacoffee.com/imicrocap](https://buymeacoffee.com/imicrocap)** — once, or
whenever you like. It is a one-time gesture, not a subscription, and it does not unlock
any additional content.

**Within 7 days of a coffee payment, we refund in full on request.** No reason needed.
After 7 days, tell us what happened; we handle refunds case by case and lean toward
granting them. Refunds are issued through Buy Me a Coffee to the original payment
method.

## Legacy: Smart Investor Tracker email alerts (paid through Zoho)

Tracker email alerts are **closed to new subscribers** as of 20 August 2026. Existing
subscribers keep receiving them for the full 365 days they paid for.

They were paid for **once, not on a renewal**, through Zoho's payment services (Zoho
Checkout and Zoho Payments). When your year ends the alerts simply stop. For these
legacy payments:

- **Within 7 days of payment, we refund in full on request**, no reason needed
- **After 7 days**, tell us what happened; we handle it case by case and lean toward
  granting refunds
- If we stop sending the alerts during a year you have paid for, **we refund the unused
  part in full, without being asked**
- The data in the alerts is the same data shown free on the tracker pages, so we do not
  refund on the basis that the information was available free; that is stated before you
  pay, and the payment is for delivery

Alert refunds are issued through **Zoho Payments** to the original payment method,
typically 5 to 10 working days depending on your bank.

## We refund in full, at any time, without being asked, if

- We charged you in error, or charged you twice
- A charge renewed after you had already cancelled
- We stop publishing during a period you have paid for

## How to ask

Email **[hello@themicrocapminute.in](mailto:hello@themicrocapminute.in)** and tell us
what the payment was for — a coffee, or a Tracker alert — and the email address used.

We reply within **three working days**. Approved refunds are issued to the original
payment method, through the same rail the payment came in on: **Buy Me a Coffee** for
coffee payments and **Zoho** for legacy Tracker alert payments. How quickly the money
appears is down to your bank or card issuer, typically **5 to 10 working days**. We
cannot speed that part up.

## What we do not refund

We do not refund on the basis that the newsletter's content did not perform as you
hoped.

**This publication is impersonal commentary. It carries no recommendations, no ratings,
no price targets and no forecasts, and it does not predict future returns.** Investment
outcomes are not something we sell, so they are not something we can refund. This is set
out in our [Terms of Service](terms.html) and in the disclaimer of every issue.

If you are dissatisfied with the quality, usefulness, or frequency of the writing
itself, that is a different matter. Tell us and we will look at it.

## Chargebacks

If something has gone wrong, please email us before raising a chargeback with your bank.
We can almost always resolve it faster.

## Complaints

If a refund decision seems wrong, reply and ask for it to be reviewed. The publisher
reads these personally.
