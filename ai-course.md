# AI Course Policy

*Last updated: 24 August 2026. This page covers the paid course only. For the
free Classroom, see [How We Use AI](ai.html).*

## The course

**Master AI Automation in 4 hours** is a hands-on course that teaches command
line, APIs, MCP servers, webhooks, and running multiple AI assistants on real
work. It includes 59 video chapters (a little over one hour of video), 59
exercise sheets, and 12 module workbooks. The name states total hands-on
learning time: about an hour of video plus at least three hours of guided
exercises.

## Price and where it is sold

The course is sold on **Udemy** at a launch price of **Rs 999**. It is the only
paid product we make. Everything else we publish, the newsletter, the Classroom,
the tracker pages, remains free.

**Udemy is the merchant of record.** Udemy processes the payment, holds your
account, provides the certificate, and handles refunds under its own 30-day
refund policy, which you can read on Udemy before you buy. We cannot issue
refunds directly, because we never touch your money. If something is wrong with
a payment or a refund, start with Udemy support; we will help you make that
case if it stalls.

## How the course is made

The production pipeline is disclosed in full on
[How We Use AI](ai.html). In short: scripts are written with AI assistance and
edited by the publisher, the narration is a synthetic voice (Shreya, from
Sarvam AI), the animations are programmatic (Manim), and every chapter is
reviewed by the publisher before release.

## What the course teaches, and what it does not

The course teaches AI skills. It contains **no investment advice, no stock
recommendations, and nothing tailored to any person**, and it never will. The
tools it names are third-party services (chatbots, model APIs, automation
platforms) that change their menus, prices and limits often; exercises are
designed to run on free tiers, but we do not control those tiers and cannot
guarantee they stay free.
