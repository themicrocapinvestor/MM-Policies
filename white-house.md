# White House Watch

*Last updated: 11 August 2026*

White House Watch is a dated index of the documented actions of the US administration,
newest first, each linked to the news report it is verified against. The page itself is
**[here](https://tracker.themicrocapminute.in/white-house.html)**, free, and updated
every morning. It holds the last thirty one days.

## Where the data comes from

The index is built from the public feed of
**[TrumpActionTracker](https://www.trumpactiontracker.info/)**, whose content is
licensed under Creative Commons CC BY-SA 4.0. We use it as journalists with credit:
this page shows a rolling thirty one day slice of dated event titles with links, a
small part of their full dataset, which their own terms allow for journalism with
attribution. We do not republish the dataset in whole or substantial part, and their
full download is theirs, not ours. The small gold tags on each action are their
domain labels (for example "Corruption", "Suppressing dissent"), shown under the same
license and credit. If you reuse this page's content, the same CC BY-SA 4.0 terms
apply: attribute, and share alike.

## Read this part first

**A listing is not a verdict.** An action appears here because it was documented in a
news report and indexed by the source. The tags are the source's editorial
categorization, not our findings, and neither is a finding of wrongdoing by a court.
Nothing on the page is investment advice. Errors and omissions may occur; the linked
news report and the source's own site are the record. We are not affiliated with or
endorsed by TrumpActionTracker or the White House.
