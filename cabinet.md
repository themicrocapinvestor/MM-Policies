# Cabinet Watch

*Last updated: 11 August 2026*

Cabinet Watch follows the public financial disclosures of the President, the Vice
President, and senior US cabinet officials, as released by the **United States Office
of Government Ethics (OGE)**. The page itself is
**[here](https://tracker.themicrocapminute.in/cabinet.html)**, free, and updated every
morning.

## What the page shows

Two lists, both covering newly posted documents in the last thirty one days. The first
is **trades**: purchases, sales, and exchanges of securities that officials report on
Periodic Transaction Reports (OGE Form 278-T), which the law requires within roughly a
month of the trade. Amounts appear exactly as the law requires filers to report them,
in ranges, not exact figures. The second is **every newly posted disclosure document**
for the tracked officials: transaction reports, annual disclosures, ethics agreements,
and compliance certifications. Every row links to the original document on the OGE
system, which is the authoritative record.

A quiet list is the expected state of this page. Senior officials commonly hold
diversified funds, which need not be reported line by line, or have divested their
holdings, so many weeks will show no trades at all.

## The special rule this data carries

Financial disclosure reports are not like the other public data we use. The Ethics in
Government Act permits obtaining and using them **only for journalism for the general
public**: in the statute's words, "by news and communications media for dissemination
to the general public". Other commercial use, credit use, and fundraising use are
prohibited by law, with civil penalties. This page is, and will remain, free public
journalism, and the data on it is never used for any other purpose. The full quotes and
our compliance notes are on the
[US Government Data](us-gov-data.html) page.

## Read this part first

**A disclosure is not an accusation.** A reported trade is exactly that: a trade,
reported as the law requires. It is not evidence of anything else, and this page does
not imply otherwise. Nothing on the page is investment advice. Errors and omissions
may occur; the linked OGE filing is the authoritative record. We are not affiliated
with or endorsed by OGE or the US Government.

## Sources

Every document is read from the Office of Government Ethics' public request system,
checked once each morning on a fixed schedule, never live. How we may reuse government
data, in the agencies' own words, is set out in
[US Government Data](us-gov-data.html), and the SEC equivalent is in
[SEC Data and Public Filings](sec-data.html).
