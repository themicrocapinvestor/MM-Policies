# Customer Support

*Last updated: 8 August 2026*

**The Microcap Minute** and **The Microcap Minute Global** are published by Ayush Agrawal.

## How to reach us

Email **[themicrocapinvestor@gmail.com](mailto:themicrocapinvestor@gmail.com)**.

This is the only support channel. We do not operate a phone line, and we do not provide
support through social media replies or direct messages.

Please include your subscription email address if your question relates to delivery or
billing, so that we can find your record.

## What we can help with

- Subscribing, unsubscribing, or changing the email address you receive issues at
- Anything to do with your Substack account or a Stripe payment
- Issues not arriving, arriving late, or landing in spam
- Billing and refunds for paid subscriptions
- Correcting a factual or numerical error in a published issue
- Questions about how the newsletter is produced, what its data sources are, or how its
  selection rule works
- Requests relating to your personal data, as described in our
  [Privacy Policy](privacy.html)
- Press, partnership, and permission enquiries

## What we cannot help with

**We cannot give you investment advice.** We cannot tell you whether to buy, sell, or
hold any security, we cannot comment on your portfolio, and we cannot answer questions
about your particular financial situation. This applies however the question is asked,
including in reply to an issue.

This is not us being unhelpful. The newsletter is impersonal commentary sent identically
to every reader, and it stays that way deliberately. Answering one reader's personal
question would change what this publication is. If you want advice specific to you,
please speak to a financial professional licensed in your own jurisdiction.

We also cannot provide historical data files, custom screens, or research on request.

## Response times

We aim to reply to every email within **three working days**. Corrections to a published
issue are treated as urgent and are usually handled the same day.

## Corrections

If you believe a figure or statement in an issue is wrong, please tell us. Include the
issue date and the specific figure.

Our figures come from third-party sources and are not independently verified against
company filings, so an error in a source will carry into our pages. When we find or are
told about a material error, we correct it and say so in the next issue.

## Complaints

If you are unhappy with how a query was handled, reply to our response and ask for it to
be reviewed. Complaints are read by the publisher personally.

## Accessibility

If any part of an issue is difficult to read or access, tell us and we will send the
content in an alternative format.
